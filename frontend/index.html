About the Model

DenseNet121 Architecture

Our model uses DenseNet121, a deep convolutional neural network known for its dense connectivity pattern, enabling maximum information flow between layers.

CheXNet Inspired

Based on the CheXNet research paper, our model is designed to detect 14 thoracic diseases from chest X-ray images with radiologist-level accuracy.

14 Disease Classes

Detects Atelectasis, Cardiomegaly, Effusion, Infiltration, Mass, Nodule, Pneumonia, Pneumothorax, Consolidation, Edema, Emphysema, Fibrosis, Pleural Thickening, and Hernia.
